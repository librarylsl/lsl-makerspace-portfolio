Our Approach

Learning Through Making

MakerSpace gives students opportunities to explore ideas, solve problems, design prototypes, use technology, test solutions and reflect on their learning.

Explore

Ask questions, investigate and discover how things work.

Create

Turn ideas into designs, prototypes and creations.

Test

Try solutions, identify problems and learn from failure.

Improve

Debug, redesign and make solutions more effective.

EXPLORE→ THINK→ DESIGN→ MAKE→ TEST→ DEBUG→ IMPROVE→ SHARE
